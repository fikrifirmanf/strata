# Code of conduct

Strata welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. This code applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces managed by the project.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and assume good intent when asking for clarification.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work.
- Respect different experiences, identities, and points of view.
- Accept corrections and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect privacy. Share only information you have permission to publish.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual attention, personal attacks, and repeated disruption are not welcome. Publishing someone's private information without permission is not allowed. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is also not allowed.

## Report a concern

Email the maintainer at [dev@fikrifirmanf.com](mailto:dev@fikrifirmanf.com). Include what happened and any relevant links or context. Avoid posting sensitive details in a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed privately. Information will be shared only as needed to understand and address the situation. Anyone involved in a report should step back from deciding its outcome.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and ask participants to change their behavior. Depending on the severity or repetition, they may issue a warning, restrict participation temporarily, or remove someone from project spaces permanently.

Decisions should account for the impact, context, and safety of the people involved. Questions or appeals can be sent to the same contact address.
